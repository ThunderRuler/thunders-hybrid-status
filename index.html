v0.5.0
Test build 0.5.0The first numbered test build: a twin-turbo V8 hybrid, plug-in drive modes, new bodies and trims, clusters on stock dials and a 3D energy monitor.Released October 7, 2026
Hybrid MAX V8Twin-turbo V8 with a parallel hybrid motor, 8-speed automatic and an electric front axle. Street, Sport and Track modes.
Plug-in drive modesEV, HV and CHG modes on the plug-in hybrids, with EV mode holding at full throttle like the real cars.
New bodies and trimsA Gen2 Prius in the Piccolina, Gavril H-Series work trucks and a Stambecco 6x6 with Toyota hybrids.
Traction modesNormal, TRAC OFF, VSC OFF and TC OFF on every hybrid, from a button or the radial menu.
Clusters on stock dialsEach body keeps its own gauges, with READY and EV lamps and a hybrid power meter where the real car has one.
3D energy monitorYour own car on screen with turning wheels, a running engine and power flowing between engine, battery and wheels.
